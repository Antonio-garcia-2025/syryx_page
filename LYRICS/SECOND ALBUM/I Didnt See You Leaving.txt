I Didn’t See You Leaving”

I didn’t see you walking out the door,
I just felt the silence hit the floor.
The air got heavy, time went slow,
And suddenly I didn’t know you anymore.

You were the light I never asked to need,
Now I’m drowning in what used to be.

And I still reach for your name at night,
Like you never left my side…

I didn’t see you leaving,
But I feel you everywhere I go.
In every shadow, every breathing,
You’re the pain I’ll never show.
I thought forever meant forever,
But forever let you go.
And now I’m falling back to pieces,
That only you could hold.

Your voice is still trapped inside my head,
Like unfinished words we never said.
I try to sleep, but I just replay
The moment love just slipped away.

And every promise that we made,
Turns into ghosts I can’t escape.

Now I’m talking to the empty air,
Pretending you are there…

I didn’t see you leaving,
But I feel you everywhere I go.
In every dream I keep repeating,
A love I can’t let go.
I thought we were unbreakable,
I guess I was wrong all along.
Now I’m learning how to live
In a world where you are gone.

If I could turn back time tonight,
I’d hold you closer, make it right.
But time doesn’t listen, it just takes,
And leaves me with what love breaks.

I didn’t see you leaving,
But I’m still waiting for your name.
Even now I’m still believing
You might whisper mine the same.
But love don’t come back when it’s over,
It just teaches you to fall…
And I’m still here in the silence,
Missing you through it all.

I didn’t see you leaving…
But I never stopped seeing you.
