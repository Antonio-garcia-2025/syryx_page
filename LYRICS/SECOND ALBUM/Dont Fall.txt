Don’t Fall 

I see the shadows in your eyes again,
You’re holding words you’re scared to say.
The night feels cold, the silence burns,
But love was real, it wasn’t a game.

If this is where our roads divide,
Just know I’m still here by your side.

Don’t fall tonight, don’t break inside,
Even if our worlds collide.
I know your heart is tired and torn,
But tomorrow still gets born.
Don’t fall tonight, just breathe and try,
Some goodbyes don’t mean goodbye.


We wrote our names in reckless dreams,
We burned too bright, we couldn’t slow.
But every scar remembers truth,
Every tear means we both felt more.


If letting go is what you need,
I’ll carry love, not the defeat.

Don’t fall tonight, don’t lose your way,
Pain doesn’t own you, it just stays.
I know it hurts to stand alone,
But you’re stronger than you know.
Don’t fall tonight, lift up your eyes,
The stars still shine behind the cries.


If you wake up and miss my name,
That doesn’t mean we failed in vain.
Some loves don’t last, but they remain,
Like songs that echo through the pain.

Don’t fall tonight, hold on somehow,
This isn’t the end, it’s just right now.
I’ll love you quiet, love you true,
Even if I’m not next to you.
Don’t fall tonight…
I’m still here, believing you.
