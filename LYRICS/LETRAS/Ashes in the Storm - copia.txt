Ashes in the Storm

I trusted you with blood on my hands,
You smiled while you buried my plans.
Every promise tasted like rust,
You fed your ego, I fed the dust.

I was fire, you were the rain,
I stood naked inside your games.
You watched me fall, you turned away,
Like my pain was just a phase.

You said my name like it meant nothing,
While my heart was slowly crushing.

I’m caught inside your fucking storm,
Spinning lies, my soul deformed.
You ripped me open, no remorse,
I’m drowning in this cyclone of force.
Your love was sharp, your truth was fake,
You left me nothing but the ache.

You laughed when I broke down cold,
Traded my heart for someone gold.
I gave you faith, you gave me hell,
A private war I couldn’t tell.

Your silence screams louder than hate,
Every second drags your weight.
You walked away clean and whole,
Left me torn — just shreds of soul.

You called it love, I call it war,
You took the hit and wanted more.

I’m caught inside your fucking storm,
Spinning lies, my soul deformed.
You ripped me open, no remorse,
I’m drowning in this cyclone of force.

I see your face when I close my eyes,
A perfect mask built on lies.
You drank my weakness, spit my name,
And walked away without the blame.

If this is love, I want it dead,
Buried deep inside my head.
I’ll carve your memory out of me,
No more chains — I break, I’m free.

I survived your violent spin,
I won’t bleed for you again.
You tried to crush me, break my core,
But I’m standing in the wreckage you left behind the door.
This storm is dying — feel the calm,
You lost control — I’ve moved on.

No tears left.
No soul to steal.
Just scars that prove
This pain was real.
