Talking to the Walls

I hear footsteps when I’m alone,
Every shadow knows my bones.
Mirrors lie, they talk back loud,
My own face wears a traitor’s mouth.

I lock the door, I check it twice,
My thoughts are rolling loaded dice.
Who’s in control? Who pulls the wire?
My brain’s a room that’s catching fire.

Calm down, breathe — that’s what they say,
But my other voice won’t go away.

I’m talking to the walls again,
They know my sins, they know my end.
One voice screams, the other laughs,
I’m split in two — no turning back.
I sweat the fear, I choke on doubt,
My head’s the war I can’t get out.

Smile for them, act normal now,
Hide the cracks somehow.
They don’t see the teeth inside,
The thoughts that crawl, the urge to bite.

I argue with myself at night,
Who’s the monster? Who’s the light?
I set the trap, I take the blame,
I’m both the hunter and the prey.

Trust no one — that includes me,
My mind’s a loaded fucking gun, you see.

I’m talking to the walls again,
They know my sins, they know my end.
One voice screams, the other laughs,
I’m split in two — no turning back.
I sweat the fear, I choke on doubt,
My head’s the war I can’t get out.

Shut up — no, listen —
You’re weak — you’re wrong —
You’ll fail — you’ll win —
This goes on and on and on!

If I smile, will they believe?
If I bleed, will I feel relieved?
I’m pacing circles in my skin,
No escape from what’s within.

I’m talking to the walls tonight,
They echo truths I try to fight.
One mind dies so one survives,
I cut the cord to stay alive.
I face myself, I stand it out,
If I’m the enemy — I’m taking me down.

Quiet now…
No voices left to hear.
Just me…
And the fear.
